\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Settings}
\label{sec:experimental_settings}

We examine overall effectiveness (\textbf{RQ1}), memory-level contributions (\textbf{RQ2}), validation mechanisms (\textbf{RQ3}), and temporal availability and decision behavior (\textbf{RQ4}).

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We evaluate five external methods:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{ProactiveAgent} uses the released evaluation prompt and proposal/null contract with the study backbone, retaining causal event--decision history capped at 1,000 events~\citep{lu2025proactiveagent,thunlp2026proactiveagentrepo}.
    \item \textbf{ContextAgent-ICL} adapts ContextAgent's in-context proactive-service judgment to public event contexts using the study backbone~\citep{yang2025contextagent}.
    \item \textbf{Mem0} uses open-source extraction, consolidation, and same-entity retrieval~\citep{chhikara2025mem0,mem0ai2026mem0v2012}.
    \item \textbf{A-MEM} constructs linked memory notes, updates their associations as events arrive, and retrieves relevant notes~\citep{xu2025amem}.
    \item \textbf{Graphiti} stores event-derived relations in a temporal graph and retrieves same-stream facts~\citep{zep2026graphiti}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Summary-memory control.}
\textbf{Cross-week Summary Memory (CSM)} recursively consolidates daily and cross-week summaries, providing a plain-summary control for retaining history beyond the recent-event window.
Its results appear beside the external baselines in Table~\ref{tab:main_results}.

\paragraph{Memory variants.}
\textbf{Event-only} supplies the current event and bounded Event Memory; \textbf{Event+Daily} additionally supplies Daily Trajectory Memory.
\textbf{Flat Retrieval Memory} augments Event-only with three similar older same-entity events on AS/AL, using fixed dense embeddings and cosine similarity.
All methods share the task semantics and final Episode Controller, with method-specific memory interfaces.
Event-only, Event+Daily, and \system{} also share their decision prompt, schema, and Event Memory bound.
CSM, A-MEM, and Graphiti augment that same event bound with their respective representations.
Appendix~\ref{app:configuration} records interface and retrieval settings.

\paragraph{Model settings.}
The primary model is \texttt{gpt-5.6-luna}, with temperature zero and thinking disabled; ProEviMem uses the official OpenAI API.
Terra/Sol comparisons replace both decision and memory-construction models (Appendix~\ref{app:backbone_sensitivity}).

\paragraph{Metrics.}
We report results separately for AS, AL, and RA using final decisions after episode control.
\textsc{Intervene} includes immediate and scheduled service; \textsc{Abstain} denotes silence at the current event.
\textbf{Precision} measures the share of interventions matching reference opportunities.
\textbf{Opportunity Detection Rate (ODR)} measures the fraction of reference-positive events identified for intervention.
\textbf{Intervention Score (IS)} is the harmonic mean of precision and ODR.
ODR and IS are task-oriented names for standard recall and F1, respectively.
Appendix~\ref{app:configuration} provides their formulas.
\textbf{False Positive Rate (FPR)}, also termed False Intervention Rate (FIR), is $\mathrm{FP}/(\mathrm{FP}+\mathrm{TN})$.
\textbf{Balanced Accuracy (BA)} averages ODR and specificity.
Higher precision, ODR, IS, and BA and lower FPR are preferred.
\textbf{Intervention Rate (IR)} is the fraction of events receiving an intervention and describes decision frequency.
