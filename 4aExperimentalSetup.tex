\section{Experimental evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

We examine overall effectiveness (\textbf{RQ1}), model choice (\textbf{RQ2}), and memory organization and validation (\textbf{RQ3}), with supplementary checks of reference sensitivity and episode behavior.

\subsection{Experimental settings}
\label{sec:experimental_settings}

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We evaluate five external methods:
\begin{list}{\labelitemi}{%
    \settowidth{\labelwidth}{\labelitemi}%
    \setlength{\labelsep}{0.5em}%
    \setlength{\leftmargin}{\labelwidth}%
    \addtolength{\leftmargin}{\labelsep}%
    \setlength{\topsep}{0pt}%
    \setlength{\partopsep}{0pt}%
    \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}%
    \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}%
}
    \item \textbf{ProactiveAgent} uses its released prompt and proposal-or-null output contract, with causal history of events and decisions capped at 1,000 events~\citep{lu2025proactiveagent,thunlp2026proactiveagentrepo}.
    \item \textbf{ContextAgent} adapts proactive-service judgment to public events~\citep{yang2025contextagent}.
    \item \textbf{Mem0} extracts and consolidates memory~\citep{chhikara2025mem0,mem0ai2026mem0v2012} for~same-entity~retrieval.
    \item \textbf{A-MEM} updates linked notes and associations for relevant-note retrieval~\citep{xu2025amem}.
    \item \textbf{Graphiti} stores temporal event relations and retrieves same-stream facts~\citep{zep2026graphiti}.
\end{list}

\paragraph{Metrics.}
Each event receives one post-controller decision; immediate and scheduled services count as \emph{intervene} at the decision event.
We report \textbf{relevance} (precision), the share of interventions matching reference opportunities; \textbf{coverage} (recall), the share of opportunities recovered; and \textbf{effectiveness} (F1), their harmonic mean.
Overall effectiveness pools confusion counts across all streams and scopes.
The \textbf{intrusion} score (false positive rate) measures the share of reference negatives receiving interventions; \textbf{balance} (balanced accuracy) averages coverage and the correct-abstention rate on reference negatives.
Higher relevance, coverage, effectiveness, and balance and lower intrusion are preferred.
The \textbf{frequency} metric measures interventions per reference-positive event: $(\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FP})/(\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FN})$, with counts pooled across streams within each scope.
Appendix~\ref{app:configuration} gives metric formulas and frequency interpretation.

\paragraph{Implementation details.}
The primary model is \texttt{gpt-5.6-luna}, with temperature zero and thinking disabled; \system{} uses the official OpenAI API.
Methods share task semantics and the intervention controller policy, with interface-specific prompts and budgets.
The event-only, event and trajectory memory, and \system{} variants share their decision prompt, schema, and context bound of 72 hours and at most 100 events, including the current event.
A-MEM and Graphiti augment that bound with their representations.
For \system{}, memory preparation and current-condition matching precede semantic screening: accepted \texttt{skip} results yield \emph{abstain}, while \texttt{escalate} results proceed to full contextual assessment followed by intervention control.
Appendix~\ref{app:configuration} lists detailed configurations.
