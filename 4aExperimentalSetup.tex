\section{Experimental evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

We examine overall effectiveness (\textbf{RQ1}), memory organization (\textbf{RQ2}), and validation mechanisms (\textbf{RQ3}), with supplementary checks of reference sensitivity, episode behavior, and model choice.

\subsection{Experimental settings}
\label{sec:experimental_settings}

\paragraph{Baselines.}

    \item \textbf{ProactiveAgent} uses its released prompt and proposal-or-null output contract, with causal history of events and decisions capped at 1,000 events~\citep{lu2025proactiveagent,thunlp2026proactiveagentrepo}.
    \item \textbf{ContextAgent} adapts in-context proactive-service judgment to public events~\citep{yang2025contextagent}.
    \item \textbf{Mem0} extracts, consolidates, and retrieves same-entity memories~\citep{chhikara2025mem0,mem0ai2026mem0v2012}.
    \item \textbf{A-MEM} constructs linked notes, updates associations, and retrieves relevant notes~\citep{xu2025amem}.
    \item \textbf{Graphiti} stores event relations in a temporal graph and retrieves same-stream facts~\citep{zep2026graphiti}.
\end{list}

\paragraph{Metrics.}
Scores use one post-controller decision per event; immediate and scheduled services count as \emph{intervene} at the decision event.
We report \textbf{relevance} (precision) as the share of interventions matching reference opportunities; \textbf{coverage} (recall) is the share of opportunities recovered.
Their harmonic mean, \textbf{effectiveness} (F1), balances relevant interventions against missed opportunities.
The \textbf{intrusion} score (false positive rate) is the share of reference negatives receiving interventions.
The \textbf{balance} score (balanced accuracy) averages coverage and the share of reference negatives correctly left without intervention.
Higher relevance, coverage, effectiveness, and balance and lower intrusion are preferred.
The \textbf{frequency} metric measures interventions per reference-positive event: $(\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FP})/(\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FN})$, with counts pooled across streams within each scope.
A value of $1\times$ indicates equal counts; larger and smaller values indicate more and fewer interventions, respectively.
This ratio describes relative intervention volume and is interpreted alongside relevance, coverage, and intrusion.

\paragraph{Implementation details.}
The primary model is \texttt{gpt-5.6-luna}, with temperature zero and thinking disabled; \system{} uses the official OpenAI API.
Methods share task semantics and the intervention controller policy, with interface-specific prompts and budgets.
The event-only, event and trajectory memory, and \system{} variants share their decision prompt, schema, and context bound of 72 hours and at most 100 events, including the current event.
A-MEM and Graphiti augment that bound with their representations.
Appendix~\ref{app:configuration} lists detailed configurations.
