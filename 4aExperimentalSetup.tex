\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

We examine overall effectiveness (\textbf{RQ1}), memory organization (\textbf{RQ2}), and validation mechanisms (\textbf{RQ3}), with supplementary checks of reference sensitivity, episode behavior, and model choice.

\subsection{Experimental Settings}
\label{sec:experimental_settings}

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We evaluate five external methods:
\begin{list}{\labelitemi}{%
    \setlength{\topsep}{0pt}%
    \setlength{\partopsep}{0pt}%
    \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}%
    \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}%
}
    \item \textbf{ProactiveAgent} uses its released prompt and proposal/null contract, with causal event--decision history capped at 1,000 events~\citep{lu2025proactiveagent,thunlp2026proactiveagentrepo}.
    \item \textbf{ContextAgent} adapts in-context proactive-service judgment to public events~\citep{yang2025contextagent}.
    \item \textbf{Mem0} extracts, consolidates, and retrieves same-entity memories~\citep{chhikara2025mem0,mem0ai2026mem0v2012}.
    \item \textbf{A-MEM} constructs linked notes, updates associations, and retrieves relevant notes~\citep{xu2025amem}.
    \item \textbf{Graphiti} stores event relations in a temporal graph and retrieves same-stream facts~\citep{zep2026graphiti}.
\end{list}

\paragraph{Implementation Details.}
The primary model is \texttt{gpt-5.6-luna}, with temperature zero and thinking disabled; \system{} uses the official OpenAI API.
Methods share task semantics and Episode Controller, with interface-specific prompts and budgets.
Event-only, Event+Daily, and \system{} share their decision prompt, schema, and 72-hour/100-event bound including the current event.
A-MEM and Graphiti augment that bound with their representations.
Appendix~\ref{app:configuration} lists detailed configurations.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
Scores use post-controller decisions; immediate and scheduled services count as \textsc{Intervene}.
\textbf{Relevance} (precision) is the share of interventions matching reference opportunities; \textbf{Coverage} (recall) is the share of opportunities recovered.
\textbf{Effectiveness} (F1), their harmonic mean, balances relevant interventions against missed opportunities.
\textbf{False Positive Rate (FPR)}, also called False Intervention Rate (FIR), is the share of reference negatives receiving interventions.
\textbf{Balance} (balanced accuracy) averages Coverage and the share of reference negatives correctly left without intervention.
Higher is preferred except for FPR.
\textbf{Intervention Rate (IR)} is the share of all events receiving interventions, without a preferred direction.
