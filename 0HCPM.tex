\begin{abstract}
Event-driven LLM agents must decide whether incoming observations warrant assistance, often using evidence spread across earlier events.
Retaining related history is not enough: the agent must assess its support and current applicability without treating recurrence alone as sufficient reason to intervene.
We propose Proactive Evidence Memory (\system{}), combining bounded recent events, source-checked daily trajectories, and replay-assessed temporal patterns.
The framework checks daily facts against their sources and assesses patterns against prior observations, then selects applicable evidence for the agent's service-opportunity judgment.
Across ProactiveStream's three application scopes, \system{} improves Intervention F1 by 4.3--11.5 percentage points over the strongest baseline in each scope, among comparators including recursive-summary and temporal-graph methods.
Hierarchy and validation ablations support the contribution of evidence organization and historical support checks to opportunity detection.
\end{abstract}
