\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

ProactiveStream covers low-risk service settings in Sports, Home, and Code under a protocol fixed after joint method and benchmark development.
Independent held-out evaluation, screening calibration, and cross-family transfer of constructed memory remain future work.
Reference labels represent service opportunities under the annotation protocol; the 450-target review assesses three selected streams.
The Code suppression diagnostic holds later states fixed; controller-free behavior requires a sequential comparison.
Historical replay assesses observed support; prospective validity under changing behavior requires further study.
Our offline metrics evaluate opportunity detection.
Recipient benefit, interruption burden, end-to-end cost, and deployment safety require prospective measurements.
The reported results support comparisons within the evaluated protocols, with broader generalization depending on additional populations, observation conditions, and model families.
