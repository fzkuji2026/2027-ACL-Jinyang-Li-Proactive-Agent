\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our six-domain protocol isolates habit induction and turn-level intervention control with fixed evidence, labels, and state progression.
It establishes reproducible mechanism-level behavior across software, smart-home, shopping, communication, scheduling, and project-management workflows.
Natural longitudinal deployments provide the next evaluation layer for evolving preferences, user-centered intervention costs, and incremental discovery timing.

Mechanism activation follows domain structure: generalized matching contributes where surface forms vary, Code exercises phase and Think checks, and Shopping exposes the cooldown operating point.
The released traces and manifests support extending the same analyses to additional domain--mechanism combinations.

External comparisons use pinned, executable artifacts under the common turn-level interface defined in Section~\ref{sec:setup}, and Table~\ref{tab:efficiency} reports provider telemetry for \system{}.
Trained end-to-end comparisons, matched latency measurements, zero-history onboarding, and user-facing habit inspection form natural extensions of the evaluated framework.
