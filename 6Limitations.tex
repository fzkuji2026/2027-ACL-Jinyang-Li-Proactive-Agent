\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

ProactiveStream covers three low-risk service scopes and 17 streams under a protocol fixed after joint method and benchmark development.
Independent held-out evaluation and cross-family model transfer remain future work.
Reference labels represent reviewed opportunities; the 450-target review assesses three selected streams, while episode judgments remain partially confirmed.
The RA suppression diagnostic holds later states fixed; controller-free behavior requires a sequential comparison.
Historical replay assesses observed support; prospective validity under changing behavior requires further study.
Our offline metrics evaluate opportunity detection.
Recipient benefit, interruption burden, end-to-end cost, and deployment safety require prospective measurements.
The reported results support comparisons within the evaluated protocols, with broader generalization depending on additional populations, observation conditions, and model families.
