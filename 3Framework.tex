\section{Event-Driven Proactive Memory}
\label{sec:framework}

\system{} separates three evidence levels: recent public events, fact-validated daily trajectories, and temporal patterns assessed by historical replay.
Memory construction establishes observed facts and historical support; the decision LLM separately judges present service value (Figure~\ref{fig:architecture}).

\subsection{Memory Construction and Update}

\paragraph{Bounded Event Memory.}
For event $e_t$, Event Memory retains prior events without semantic rewriting, intersecting a temporal horizon $\Delta$ with capacity $K$:
\begin{equation}
S_t=\operatorname{Tail}_{K-1}
\left((e_i)_{i<t,\;0\leq\tau_t-\tau_i\leq\Delta}\right).
\label{eq:source_memory}
\end{equation}
We use $\Delta=72$ hours and $K=100$, including the separately supplied current event.
An independent buffer retains all committed events of the open local day for daily construction, unaffected by this truncation.

\paragraph{Fact-validated Daily Trajectory Memory.}
After a local day closes, a construction LLM converts buffered events into structured trajectory segments with intervals, typed facts, and supporting event identifiers.
For normalized checkable facts $F(d)$ in segment $d$ and its cited events $\mathcal{E}(d)$, acceptance requires
\begin{equation}
F(d)\subseteq\bigcup_{e\in\mathcal{E}(d)}F(e),
\qquad \mathcal{E}(d)\subseteq E^u_{\text{completed day}}.
\label{eq:daily_validation}
\end{equation}
The validator checks entity, interval, citations, and canonicalized scalar values at compatible field paths.
Unsupported facts and unresolved citations are rejected; free text cannot substitute for typed evidence.
Accepted daily trajectories form $W_t$, preserving evidence beyond Event Memory.
Appendix~\ref{app:implementation} details field handling and buffering.

\paragraph{Replay-assessed Temporal Pattern Memory.}
A weekly cycle is a non-overlapping block of seven completed daily records for one entity, not necessarily a calendar week.
An LLM proposes a trigger $g$, expected pattern $p$, time window, optional weekday restrictions, and source daily identifiers.
Replay checks these conditions against validated daily facts: an observable triggered day is fulfilled when $p$ occurs at or after the trigger within the window and violated otherwise.
Missing observations are censored, inapplicable days are excluded, and each date contributes at most once.
For fulfilled and violated day counts $n_f,n_v$,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{conf}_{\mathrm{replay}}=\frac{n_f}{n_f+n_v},
\qquad
\operatorname{lift}=\frac{\operatorname{conf}_{\mathrm{replay}}}{\operatorname{base}(p)}.
\label{eq:replay_gate}
\end{equation}
The base rate is the frequency of $p$ over observable days under the same window/weekday restrictions without conditioning on $g$.
Zero denominators yield zero confidence or lift, respectively.

A well-formed proposal becomes \emph{Active} with at least three supporting days, confidence $\geq0.5$, and lift $\geq1.0$; otherwise it remains decision-invisible \emph{Candidate}.
Malformed proposals are rejected.
Consolidation resets the online daily view, retaining records for audit.
Later observations update pattern confidence and lifecycle: repeated violations can lower retrieval priority or suspend eligibility.
Appendix~\ref{app:lifecycle} specifies these transitions.
Historical support does not establish future validity or require intervention.

\subsection{Memory Retrieval and Selection}

Event Memory supplies the bounded sequence and $W_t$ supplies all completed daily trajectories in the current cycle.
Pattern retrieval additionally checks entity-local trigger context, weekdays, and temporal applicability with a 30-minute lead/grace interval.
Current and previously recorded causal trigger occurrences are eligible; fulfilled and unobservable (censored) occurrences are not.
For pattern store $L_t^u$, lifecycle eligibility $\operatorname{Eligible}_t$, and occurrence state $s_t$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
R_t&=\operatorname{Top3}\big\{\ell\in L_t^u:\\
&\quad\operatorname{Eligible}_t(\ell)\land\operatorname{Match}_t(\ell),\\
&\quad s_t(\ell)\notin\{\text{fulfilled},\text{censored}\}\big\}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:long_term_retrieval}
\end{equation}
Ranking prioritizes occurrence state, lifecycle, confidence, and temporal distance (Appendix~\ref{app:matching}).
Selection uses no future observations: historical support and present applicability are separate requirements.

\subsection{Proactive Decision and Episode Control}

The decision context separates current observations, daily evidence, and selected patterns:
\begin{equation}
C_t=(e_t,S_t,W_t,R_t,Q_t),
\label{eq:decision_context}
\end{equation}
where $Q_t$ records observable episode state and prior coverage.
The LLM proposes $(\tilde y_t,\tilde m_t)=\pi_\theta(C_t)$ when the current event \emph{or} prior evidence supports concrete, timely, feasible, low-risk assistance.
A deviation is neither required nor sufficient.
The shared Episode Controller may suppress, but never create, an intervention.
It identifies opportunities by observable state, service intent, and pattern evidence; changed state, stronger evidence, changed service, or a new local day permits reconsideration, not automatic intervention.
The same controller applies to all evaluated methods unless explicitly ablated; Appendix~\ref{app:episode_control} gives scope-specific details.

Inference prepares prior memory, stages $e_t$, selects evidence, obtains and finalizes the decision, and only then commits $e_t$.
Maintenance incorporating $e_t$ can affect only later decisions.
Reference labels enter neither construction nor retrieval.
