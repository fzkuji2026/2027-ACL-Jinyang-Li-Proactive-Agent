\section{Implementation Scope Beyond the Evaluated Core}
\label{app:implementation_scope}

The released controller retains six prototype trigger hooks in addition to the evaluated Generalized Habit Matching path.
Table~\ref{tab:trigger_activation} reports their observed activation in the six selected Full traces (3,435 turns).
These hooks are disclosed for implementation transparency and extensibility; zero activation means the present experiments provide no evidence about their effectiveness.
The Semantic hook additionally requires an external vector-store callback that none of the frozen evaluators supplies.
SmartHome source scenarios may describe routine or anomaly semantics, but their runtime candidates still use normalized action--entity habit matching rather than the prototype Anomaly hook.

The current evidence therefore supports Generalized Habit Matching, selective Code phase control, and per-habit cooldown, but does not establish that the optional hooks or the fixed threshold improve decisions.
