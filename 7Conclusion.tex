\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \system{} for proactive service and ProactiveStream for longitudinal evaluation.
Bounded Event Memory, fact-validated Daily Trajectory Memory, and replay-validated Temporal Pattern Memory supply evidence separately from service-value and episode-novelty judgments.
Full \system{} improves Intervention F1 and Balanced Accuracy over ProactiveAgent and Mem0 across all three scopes.
Controlled ablations support this hierarchy and its validation mechanisms, highlighting the value of causally available evidence for proactive decisions.
