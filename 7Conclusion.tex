\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced ProactiveStream to evaluate proactive assistance over event streams as history accumulates.
\system{} combines recent events, source-checked temporal trajectories, and replay-assessed behavioral regularities, bringing evidence from earlier observations into current decisions.
Across Sports, Home, and Code, it achieves higher effectiveness and balance than the evaluated baselines.
The memory ablations further show improved opportunity recovery with trajectories and fewer false interventions with the full hierarchy.
These results support maintaining historical evidence at multiple temporal scales for \mbox{event-driven proactive assistance}.
