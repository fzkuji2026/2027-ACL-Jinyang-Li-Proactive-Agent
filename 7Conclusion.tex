\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \system{}, a proactive memory framework that combines bounded recent events, source-checked daily trajectories, and replay-assessed temporal patterns.
Across the three scopes of ProactiveStream, \system{} achieves higher Effectiveness and Balance than the evaluated baselines and cross-week summary control.
Ablations support the contributions of hierarchical organization, daily fact validation, and historical replay gating.
These findings highlight the value of matching source-grounded, historically supported memory to current observations for service-opportunity detection.
