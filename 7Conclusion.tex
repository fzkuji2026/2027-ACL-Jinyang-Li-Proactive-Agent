\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \system{}, which turns longitudinal observations into source-checked daily trajectories and replay-assessed temporal patterns for proactive service decisions.
On ProactiveStream, it improves Intervention F1 and Balanced Accuracy over four baselines across three scopes; ablations support the hierarchy and its evidence checks.
The resulting design treats history as traceable evidence with temporal applicability, while leaving present service value and opportunity novelty to the decision process.
