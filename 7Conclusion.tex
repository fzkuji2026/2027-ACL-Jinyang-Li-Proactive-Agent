\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \system{}, a proactive memory framework that combines bounded recent events, source-checked temporal trajectories, and replay-assessed behavioral regularities.
Across the three scopes of ProactiveStream, \system{} achieves higher event-level effectiveness and balance than the evaluated external baselines.
Ablations support the contributions of hierarchical organization, trajectory fact validation, and historical replay gating.
These findings highlight the value of matching source-grounded, historically supported memory to current observations for service-opportunity detection.
