\paragraph{Comparisons with external methods.}
The IS advantage spans proactive prompting, extracted memories, linked notes, and temporal-graph retrieval.
On AS, \system{} attains the lowest FPR among all comparators; on AL, it combines the highest precision and ODR.
Relative to A-MEM, it recovers additional reference opportunities while reducing false interventions in every scope.
These results support selecting historical information that is applicable to the current service need.
The comparisons assess complete memory interfaces; the controlled ablations below examine individual design choices.

\paragraph{Cross-week summary control.}
CSM improves on Event-only in every scope, showing the value of retaining compressed history beyond the recent-event window.
\system{} further improves IS and precision across all three scopes.
On RA, \system{} retains 89.7\% ODR while producing 179 fewer false interventions than CSM, with nine fewer true positives.
This trade-off yields an 11.5-point IS gain, supporting more selective use of retained history.
Section~\ref{sec:validation_ablations} tests how source validation and replay gating contribute to this selectivity.

\subsection{Temporal Memory and Decision Behavior}
\label{sec:validation_results}
\label{sec:case_analysis}

\paragraph{When longer history becomes usable.}
The AS/AL audit records 423 daily trajectories and 42 Active temporal patterns.
Median times to first daily trajectory, Active pattern, and relevant pattern are 0.80, 6.79, and 10.46 days, calculated over 13, 11, and eight entities reaching the respective stages.
These stage-specific summaries describe construction, admission, and first online matching across distinct eligible-entity sets.
They motivate preserving longitudinal history for both memory construction and subsequent use (RQ4).
Appendix~\ref{app:construction_audit} gives the full counts and eligible-entity denominators.

\paragraph{From historical pattern to current judgment.}
A recorded AS case links five days of STILL observations, completed daily trajectories, and an activated afternoon pattern.
At a later matched event, the participant has 778 recorded steps in the preceding hour; the decision model returns \textsc{Abstain}, which the controller preserves.
The current activity provides context for interpreting the earlier pattern.
This case illustrates how applicable memory informs service-value judgment while the final decision responds to the current observation.
Appendix~\ref{app:case_examples} traces the timestamps and source links.

\paragraph{Intervention frequency and selectivity.}
For reference-positive prevalence $\rho$, the identity $\mathrm{IR}=\rho\,\mathrm{ODR}+(1-\rho)\,\mathrm{FPR}$ separates opportunity recovery from false intervention.
AL and RA have similar prevalences, yet ProactiveAgent intervenes much more frequently on RA.
Together with the main comparisons, this shows that intervention frequency reflects both the service setting and the method's selectivity.
The hierarchy improves this balance by conditioning decisions on supported, currently applicable history.

\paragraph{Additional comparisons.}
Flat Retrieval Memory improves on Event-only in AS/AL, while \system{} achieves higher precision and ODR than this retrieval control.
Replacing both decision and construction LLMs from Luna to Terra to Sol also improves IS and FPR in every scope (Appendix~\ref{app:backbone_sensitivity}).

\paragraph{Reference-label and episode diagnostics.}
A separate three-reviewer team assesses 150 consecutive targets per scope with complete prior history and model outputs hidden; label-version agreement with the earlier references ranges from 85.3\% to 91.3\%.
Under the reviewed labels, \system{} has higher IS than CSM in all three scopes; the small AS ordering changes between label versions (Appendix~\ref{app:label_review_450}).
The provisional paired episode diagnostic distinguishes first assistance, valuable follow-up, and repetition, characterizing coverage and selectivity under a shared opportunity inventory (Appendix~\ref{app:episode_review_450}).
