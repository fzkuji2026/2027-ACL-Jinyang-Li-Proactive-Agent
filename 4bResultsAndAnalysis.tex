\paragraph{Coverage and selectivity.}
The gains do not reflect uniformly more intervention.
On AS, \system{} reduces FIR against all four baselines, with less recall than ProactiveAgent and Mem0.
On AL, it improves both precision and recall over all baselines; FIR increases relative to ProactiveAgent and Mem0 but decreases relative to CSM and Graphiti.
On RA, it recovers more opportunities with lower FIR than ProactiveAgent, while Mem0's infrequent interventions leave many opportunities uncovered.
Intervention Rate is thus an operating point, not a performance target.
ProactiveAgent intervenes on 9.2\% of AL events versus 53.1\% of RA events despite similar reference-positive prevalences (11.7\% versus 11.9\%).
Appendix~\ref{app:rate_interpretation} decomposes this difference without attributing it solely to the LLM.

\paragraph{Summary and graph memory.}
CSM exceeds Event-only by 4.9, 11.8, and 15.2 F1 points on AS, AL, and RA, supporting plain-summary retention.
\system{} remains ahead of CSM by 5.5, 6.8, and 11.5 points.
On RA, CSM recovers nine more reference opportunities (165 versus 156 TP), but incurs 179 more FP (412 versus 233); ProEviMem's advantage is selectivity rather than maximum coverage.
Graphiti reaches 40.3\%, 40.4\%, and 37.7\% F1: temporal relation retrieval does not match ProEviMem under this adapter.
These memory-interface comparisons do not isolate replay's contribution; Section~\ref{sec:validation_ablations} tests it directly.

\paragraph{Retrieval and model controls.}
Flat Retrieval Memory attains 41.3\% F1 on AS and 48.2\% on AL, below \system{} by 7.4 and 10.4 points despite similar intervention rates.
\system{} has higher precision and recall in both scopes.
Jointly replacing decision and construction LLMs from Luna to Terra to Sol increases F1 and decreases FIR in every scope (RQ4; Appendix~\ref{app:backbone_sensitivity}).

\paragraph{Evidence checks.}
Removing replay gating increases Intervention Rate and reduces precision, recall, and F1 in all scopes.
Removing daily validation also lowers precision/F1, while recall is unchanged on AS/RA and slightly higher on AL.
On RA, retaining replay changes 145 TP/284 FP to 156 TP/233 FP; daily validation retains 156 TP while reducing FP from 276 to 233.
The larger F1 loss without replay appears in every scope, supporting both evidence checks beyond store consistency (RQ3).

\paragraph{Episode control.}
On the recorded RA sequence, episode suppression changes 173 TP/299 FP to 156 TP/233 FP: 66 fewer FP but 17 fewer TP.
Precision and F1 rise by 3.5 and 1.9 points, while recall falls from 99.4\% to 89.7\% and BA from 88.1\% to 85.8\%.
For these recorded proposals, suppression trades coverage for precision/F1 and can withhold reference-positive interventions.

\subsection{Evidence Availability and Error Analysis}
\label{sec:validation_results}
\label{sec:case_analysis}

\paragraph{Construction and availability.}
Across 13 AS/AL entities, 423 daily trajectories contain 1,279 source-supported segments; logs record 311 rejected checks and no retained cross-entity evidence.
The pattern store has 42 Active and 12 Candidate proposals with replay-consistent records (Appendix~\ref{app:construction_audit}).
Median times to first daily evidence, Active pattern, and relevant pattern are 0.80, 6.79, and 10.46 days among the 13, 11, and eight entities reaching each stage, respectively.
Activation therefore does not ensure current relevance.

A logged AS timeline links source events, daily extraction, pattern admission, and a matched context ending in ABSTAIN (Appendix~\ref{app:case_examples}).
It illustrates evidence flow and service-value judgment (RQ4), not counterfactual memory necessity.

\paragraph{Human-review diagnostic.}
A separate three-reviewer team, blinded to model outputs, reviews one contiguous 150-event segment per scope with its complete preceding history.
Agreement with earlier diagnostic references is 88.7\%, 91.3\%, and 85.3\% in AS, AL, and RA.
Holding each method's decisions fixed, reviewed-label F1 is 42.5\%/55.6\%/47.4\% for \system{} versus 41.0\%/37.2\%/46.5\% for CSM (Appendix~\ref{app:label_review_450}).
The AL/RA ordering is preserved, but the small AS difference reverses: most labels agree, yet opportunity boundaries affect this sampled comparison.

\paragraph{Episode diagnostic.}
A provisional paired analysis of CSM and \system{} separates first assistance, valuable follow-up, and redundancy on the same 450 targets (Appendix~\ref{app:episode_review_450}).
Under the shared proposed references, \system{} has higher supported-intervention precision across scopes, but CSM covers more RA opportunities; this diagnoses coverage--selectivity trade-offs rather than uniform superiority.
